## Numbers

- **Contrast (LRV):** >= 30 points between critical surfaces (floor/wall, wall/door, wall/handrail). DSDC-LRV "minimum of 30 point difference"; TPT; BS 8300 / Approved Doc M; origin Bright, Cook & Harris (Reading). BS 8300-2:2018 (secondary): 20 acceptable for large areas above 200 lux; below 20 poor.
- **Adjoining floors:** "maximum of 10 LRV point difference, but ideally less than eight" (DSDC-LRV): floor-transition threshold.
- **Lux at task areas (DSDC):** living 300, kitchen 600, bathroom 300, bedroom 200, hall 100-150, stairs 150; adjacent areas >= half; CRI >= 85; older people need about twice a younger person's light. TPT: up to 1000 for some tasks.
- **Signs:** about 1.2 m from floor (DSDC, KF).
- **LRV from a photo:** linearise sRGB, Y = 0.2126R + 0.7152G + 0.0722B, LRV ~ 100Y. Compare only adjacent lit surfaces in the same frame (auto exposure). Bands: < 20 flag, 20-30 borderline, >= 30 OK. A photo cannot measure lux: present dim lighting as advice.

## Wording corrections for the current 10

- **dark_rug:** it's tone against the surrounding floor, doormats included; fix = mat the same tone as the floor (DSDC). Strong contrast either way is flagged by KF; dark on light is worst.
- **loose_rug:** camera can't confirm looseness; call it "throw rug / mat (trip risk)". ASH: remove "especially if they are frayed or slide".
- **patterned_floor:** "textured or deeper", insects, litter. "Looks like a step" belongs to floor-type changes (row 6), not busy patterns.
- **mirror:** hedge: ASH "may also start to become confusing. If so..."; DSDC: mirrors "are appreciated by most people with dementia". Advise covering only if it causes distress.
- **stair_edge_hard_to_see:** DSDC: contrasting nosing alone may not be visible from above; don't suggest contrasting flooring at the top of stairs.
- **toilet_lookalike:** supported, but only by ASH.
- **bathroom_fitting_blends_in:** seat should contrast with the pan and the floor; cistern with the wall (DSDC).
- **Fixes that add a rug** (e.g. cover a patterned floor) conflict with remove-rugs advice: say flat, rubber-backed, same-tone runner.
- **Don't name one best colour** (DUK: blue easier to see than red; others say red).
